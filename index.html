Course Evaluation

CSE326: Internet Programming Syllabus & Practicals Mapping

FitBuddy by WebWeavers demonstrates practical mastery of Units I to VI with real, functional APIs:

Unit I: HTML Fundamentals & Semantic Web
  • Structure & Typography: Semantic DOCTYPE, meta viewport, charset, headings, lists, tables with thead/tbody/tfoot.
  • SVG Graphics: Inline interactive SVG icons, animated SVG calorie progress ring, dynamic SVG weekly calorie bar chart.
  • Diet & Nutrition Engine: Day-wise 7-day meal planner with Veg & Non-Veg macro tracking, web data extraction, and JSON sync.
Unit II & III: CSS3 Styling & Responsive Layouts
  • Orange & White Design: CSS custom properties (variables), clean contrast, glassmorphism.
  • CSS Grid & Flexbox: Multi-column dashboard grid, 7-day schedule grid, responsive nav bar.
  • Animations & Print: Smooth transitions, hover cards, keyframe pulse animations, print media stylesheet.
Unit IV: JavaScript Core & DOM Manipulation
  • ES6+ Standards: Arrow functions, destructuring, template literals, Map/Set, Promises, async/await.
  • Form Validation: Real-time regex pattern testing, password complexity meter, numerical range checks.
  • State Management: Centralized state engine, dynamic table filtering, sorting, and inline editing.
Unit V: Advanced Web APIs (HTML5 API Suite)
  • Web Storage API: Persistent multi-user accounts dictionary and active session in localStorage.
  • Geolocation API: Real-time watchPosition telemetry with Haversine distance and MET calorie math.
  • HTML5 Drag & Drop API: Drag exercises from pool and reorder day-wise workout routines.
  • Web Audio API: Synthesized audio tones for workout start, pause, interval countdown, and completion.
Unit VI: Web Application Deployment & Modern Standards
  • PWA & Service Worker: Manifest.json and sw.js for caching and installability.
  • Leaflet & OpenStreetMap: Real interactive live GPS map with online satellite/street tiles.
  • Role-Based Access: Athlete vs Coach/Staff admin control center and roster management.
  • Deployment: 100% client-side, zero build step, immediately deployable on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or local PowerShell server.

Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

1. Zero-Telemetry & Client Privacy

FitBuddy by WebWeavers is an athlete-first, privacy-respecting platform. All authentication records, workout history, GPS runs, and custom diet plans are strictly stored inside your device's browser localStorage. No telemetry or sensitive personal metrics are transmitted to third-party ad networks.

2. Health & Athletic Safety

Exercises and nutrition guidelines in FitBuddy are curated based on International Society of Sports Nutrition (ISSN) and National Institutes of Health (NIH) frameworks. Always warm up properly and consult a physician before undertaking high-intensity physical exertion.

3. Data Portability

You own 100% of your data. You may export your activity log to CSV or JSON, download the entire Day-Wise Diet Plan, or delete your account at any time.
